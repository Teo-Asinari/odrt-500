Scaling laws are fitted to seven cited Tecnotion frameless motors (`data/motor_family.csv`), and the sized candidates are run through the same drivetrain model and heavy scenarios (A-023). Winding limit 140 °C; mount-to-air 1.0 K/W with 2000 J/K of mount heat capacity (placeholders).

## Reading this

- The winding-to-mount thermal path matters as much as Km. The cited frameless motors (stator potted into a housing) shed winding heat far better than the RO100 as modelled (A-015, A-022). A custom motor only gets these numbers if it is built the same way.
- Off-the-shelf frameless parts already sit in the useful range. QTR-A-105-60 (Km ≈ 0.63, 1.9 kg) is close to the 105 mm / Km 0.6 candidate, and QTL-A-210-85 (Km ≈ 3.7, 8.3 kg) is close to direct drive at 210 mm / Km 3.5. Their listed windings are far too slow at 48 V, and prices are not stated, so a custom winding or a higher bus voltage would be needed.
- Direct drive cuts felt inertia (about 11-26 kg versus 31-91 kg geared) and removes the belt, but needs a 6-13 kg motor and roughly 40-60 N·m. Not modelled: speed and voltage limits, magnet cost, manufacturability.
