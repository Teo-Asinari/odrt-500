## H-01 — Handle release under load (slingshot) (S4)

- **Cause:** User lets go or the grip slips at high tension.
- **Effect:** The cable's stored elastic energy (~F^2/2k) launches the handle toward the device. Separately, the controller can wind the drum hard into the end stop.
- **Mitigations:**
  - Release detection drops command to rest tension (firmware
  - simulated)
  - Retraction speed limiter (firmware
  - simulated)
  - Mechanical handle arrestor or damper at the cable exit (mechanical
  - not designed)
  - Stiffer cable to cut stored energy (mechanical
  - trade study needed)
- **Requirements:** R-08, R-09

## H-02 — Load cell fails low (stuck at zero) (S3)

- **Cause:** Sensor, wiring or ADC fault.
- **Effect:** Unmonitored, the PI loop drives tension above the command. Monitored, the fault also looks like a handle release, so the load drops and the user lurches.
- **Mitigations:**
  - Load-cell vs motor-model disagreement monitor (firmware
  - simulated)
  - Redundant tension sensing (independent-hardware
  - not designed)
  - Ramp-down instead of drop on detected faults (firmware
  - not simulated)
- **Requirements:** R-09

## H-03 — Loss of mains power mid-pull (S3)

- **Cause:** Outage, unplugging, PSU failure.
- **Effect:** With the phases left open, resistance disappears within tens of ms (only drum inertia remains) and the user lurches. With the phases shorted, braking is proportional to speed.
- **Mitigations:**
  - Normally-closed phase-short relay on power loss (independent-hardware
  - simulated as upper bound)
  - Hold-up energy for a controlled ramp-down (independent-hardware
  - not designed)
- **Requirements:** R-06, R-09

## H-04 — Regenerative overvoltage (S3)

- **Cause:** Braking resistor or chopper missing, failed or undersized. The supply cannot absorb energy.
- **Effect:** The bus passes 60 V within a fraction of a second of a heavy pull, which can destroy the electronics and leave torque uncontrolled.
- **Mitigations:**
  - Braking chopper and resistor sized for peak regen (independent-hardware
  - simulated)
  - Hardware overvoltage crowbar or drive disable (independent-hardware
  - not designed)
  - Energy buffer (independent-hardware
  - trade study)
- **Requirements:** R-06, R-09

## H-05 — Supply undervoltage, torque dropout (S3)

- **Cause:** Motor draw exceeds supply power while lowering heavy loads.
- **Effect:** The drive locks out torque, so resistance is lost mid-rep.
- **Mitigations:**
  - Larger supply or energy buffer (independent-hardware
  - trade study)
  - Graceful torque derating before lockout (firmware
  - not simulated)
- **Requirements:** R-06

## H-06 — Stuck-on or runaway torque (controller crash, wrong sign) (S4)

- **Cause:** Firmware hang, a wrong torque sign after a configuration error, or a drive fault.
- **Effect:** Up to full stall tension, which is far above the commanded load, or the cable reels in toward the device.
- **Mitigations:**
  - Independent watchdog that removes drive enable (independent-hardware
  - not designed)
  - Independent tension and speed limits in a separate safety MCU or hardware comparator (independent-hardware
  - not designed)
  - Torque-limited motor and gearing choice (mechanical; stall tension in structure.md)
- **Requirements:** R-09

## H-07 — Encoder or speed-sensor failure (S3)

- **Cause:** Encoder loss or a bad reading.
- **Effect:** Friction and inertia compensation, the speed limiter and release detection all degrade or misbehave.
- **Mitigations:**
  - Encoder plausibility checks against motor back-EMF (firmware
  - not designed)
- **Requirements:** R-09

## H-08 — Motor overtemperature (S2)

- **Cause:** Sustained high tension (500 lbf holds) or blocked cooling.
- **Effect:** Winding damage, demagnetisation, torque loss.
- **Mitigations:**
  - Thermal model in firmware plus winding thermistor (firmware)
  - Duty-cycle limits on peak tension (firmware)
- **Requirements:** R-07

## H-09 — Cable or termination failure (S4)

- **Cause:** Wear, fatigue, sharp bends at the fairlead, overload from snap loads.
- **Effect:** Sudden release (as H-01, plus whipping cable).
- **Mitigations:**
  - Rope rated at 5x design tension (mechanical; see structure.md)
  - Bend radius and wear inspection rules (mechanical
  - not defined)
  - Cable guard (mechanical
  - not designed)
- **Requirements:** R-08

## H-10 — Anchor or mount failure (S4)

- **Cause:** Wall or floor anchor pull-out, or an under-rated substrate.
- **Effect:** The device detaches under load.
- **Mitigations:**
  - Anchor bolts sized for stall plus snap loads (mechanical; first pass in structure.md)
  - Rated mounting bracket and installation instructions (mechanical
  - not designed)
- **Requirements:** R-08

## H-11 — Slack cable and snap at low force (S2)

- **Cause:** Reflected inertia overruns the user at low set tension, so the cable goes slack and then snaps taut.
- **Effect:** Jerky resistance, impact loads, poor control below about 20 lbf.
- **Mitigations:**
  - Lower reflected inertia (drivetrain choice)
  - Better state estimation or higher-bandwidth sensing (firmware)
  - Slack detection with speed-mode take-up (firmware
  - not simulated)
- **Requirements:** R-03, R-04

## H-12 — Entanglement or pinch points (S4)

- **Cause:** Fingers, hair or clothing reaching the drum, belts or fairlead.
- **Effect:** Crushing, amputation, entanglement.
- **Mitigations:**
  - Enclosure and guards on belts
  - drum and fairlead (mechanical
  - not designed)
  - Speed and torque limits (firmware)
- **Requirements:** R-09

## H-13 — Stored electrical energy (S2)

- **Cause:** Bus capacitors or an energy buffer remain charged after shutdown.
- **Effect:** Shock or arc during service.
- **Mitigations:**
  - Bleed resistors and a discharge indicator (independent-hardware
  - not designed)
- **Requirements:** R-06, R-09
