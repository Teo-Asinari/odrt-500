## Standalone safety and two-unit operation

- Each unit needs local tension/speed sensing, watchdog, stop input, torque disable and power-loss braking. These cannot depend on a peer unit or board occupancy.
- Board mode additionally needs authenticated adapter retention and board presence. Current deck-empty fault simulations are an ideal board-presence abstraction only.
- Wall/rack mode must require a qualified adapter and installation; absence of a board must not be treated as permission to bypass retention checks. Mode detection and its fault behavior are not implemented.
- Shared-bar operation needs independent stops plus a defined coordinated response when one unit loses power, sensing or communication. No validated two-unit safety protocol exists.
- Snap-load arrestor, rope selection/fatigue, enclosure pinch guards, EMC and mains isolation remain open.
